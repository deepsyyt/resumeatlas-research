# Sources

Where the numbers in this field come from — including the ones that are not ours.

- [Recycled statistics](recycled-statistics.md) — a provenance audit of the
  figures that dominate resume and ATS advice, and how well each one holds up

## Why this directory exists

While collecting the [questions people ask](../questions/), one pattern turned
out to be stronger than any individual question: a small number of statistics
govern the entire topic space, they are repeated across hundreds of pages, and
several of them cannot be traced to a published methodology.

Recording that is useful in itself. It also sets the standard this repository
holds itself to — every figure we publish carries its sample size, its window and
its limitations, precisely because the field's existing numbers do not.

## Our own provenance

Every number published here traces to a dataset in [`datasets/`](../datasets/),
generated by a script that runs against the production database and stamps its
own `generatedAt`, window and sample size. Method and limits are in
[`methodology/`](../methodology/).
