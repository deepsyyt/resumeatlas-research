# Cluster: keywords

**The core confusion.** Which words to include, how many, and whether keyword
matching is what actually decides an application.

## Recurring themes

- Which keywords belong on a resume, and where they should sit
- How many keywords are enough, and whether there is such a thing as too many
- Whether keyword lists differ by industry or role
- Which terms are treated as filler and ignored
- What AI-based screening looks for, as distinct from older keyword matching

## Research status: open

We hold relevant data but have not published a study on this cluster.

What exists so far is indirect. The [skill gap analysis](../research/skill-gap-analysis.md)
shows which terms postings ask for and resumes lack, and it contains one finding
that speaks to this cluster: terms describing ways of working — agile,
communication, stakeholder management — are matched constantly and missing almost
never. They are close to universally claimed, which makes them close to useless as
a differentiator. Adding more of them cannot close a gap that is not there.

A proper keyword study would need to address density, placement and section, none
of which we currently measure.

## What we will not claim

That some keyword count is optimal. We have no outcome data, so any "include N
keywords" figure from us would be exactly the kind of unsourced number this
repository exists to avoid — see
[recycled statistics](../sources/recycled-statistics.md).
