# Cluster: resume writing

**The core confusion.** What a bullet point should actually contain — and whether
the standard advice to quantify everything is realistic.

## Recurring themes

- Whether bullet points need numbers, and what to do when none are available
- How many bullets a role should have, and how long each should be
- Which formula to follow for a bullet
- How to quantify achievements in roles that produce no obvious metrics
- What to leave off a resume entirely
- How much attention a resume actually receives

## What we can answer

[Bullet quantification](../research/resume-bullet-quantification.md) measures the
gap between the advice and the practice: the median resume carries a substantial
number of bullets and quantifies about one in twenty, and a quarter contain no
number anywhere.

The interesting part is not that people ignore the advice. It is that quantifying
requires a figure the writer often was never given — which makes "add numbers"
cheap to say and expensive to follow, especially earlier in a career.

## What we cannot answer

Whether quantified bullets perform better. No outcome data exists here, so the
question this cluster most wants answered — does it work — is out of reach.

We also cannot speak to bullet length, count per role, or formatting conventions;
none of that is measured.
